SECRET is a cheerleading sports club in Odesa, Ukraine. Seven teams, from four-year-olds to adult athletes.

Training three times a week in two halls on Akademichna Street, Odesa.

From first skills to the national team

Founded on 15 August 2018. SECRET is an official member of the All-Ukrainian Cheerleading Federation, a member of the International Cheer Union (ICU) recognised by the IOC — so our athletes can compete for Ukraine at European and World Championships. Founder and head coach: Kristina Yukhimchak.

Sign up for training

Leave your phone and your child’s age. The club administrator will call you, place your child in a team by age and level, and agree on the first training.

  1. 1

    Request

    Your name, phone and your child’s age — nothing more.

  2. 2

    Call

    The club administrator calls you and picks a team and a time.

  3. 3

    First training

    • 2 Akademichna St, Odesa
    • 30 Akademichna St, Odesa

The club app

For parents, athletes and coaches. For club members only: the first sign-in uses an invitation code from the club administrator, after that an SMS code. We are preparing it for the App Store first, then for Google Play.
